Longmen Four Inscriptions

The strength of stone inscriptions, brought to the board.

The Longmen Four Inscriptions are four celebrated Northern Wei Buddhist dedicatory inscriptions at the Longmen Grottoes in Luoyang. These single-character pieces echo their angular strokes, firm structures, and archaic strength through carved lettering.
