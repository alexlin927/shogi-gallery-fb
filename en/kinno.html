Jin Nong

Broad horizontals, incisive fine lines.

Qing-dynasty artist Jin Nong developed the distinctive “lacquer script”: broad, flat horizontals contrast with fine verticals and sweeps. In this set, bold king characters and substantial strokes in the lance and knight are balanced by lively fine lines.
