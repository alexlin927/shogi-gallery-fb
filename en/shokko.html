Shokkō

Bold strokes, expressive forms.

Shokkō is associated with the eighth-generation Komagon of Osaka. Its kings and promoted pawn have distinctive forms. Here, substantial strokes meet delicate curves, with dark lettering standing clearly against warm wood.
