Gyoryū · Single Character

A world of movement in a single character.

Single-character Gyoryū gives each symbol room to breathe. Kings, rook, and bishop reveal the contrast between strong strokes and flowing lines. This set bears the maker’s inscription 阿震作, shown alongside photographs of the pieces.
