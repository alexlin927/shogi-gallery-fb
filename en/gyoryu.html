Gyoryū

Supple lines with a quiet strength.

Gyoryū is a lettering style created by calligrapher Shigeki Uemura. Fine extended lines balance confident strokes. The kings feel composed, while the rook and promoted rook convey movement. Wood grain and carved shadows give each piece its own expression.
